# filter/reverb wrap=clamp resolution — pass 28, 2026-09-27

Root cause of the `filter__reverb__wrap__clamp` pixel-unresolved divergence
(GAP-001 pass 27): the pixel gates' goldens come from the reference WEBGL2
backend (`parity/batch-golden.mjs --backend webgl2`), whose GLSL
(`shaders/effects/filter/reverb/glsl/reverb.glsl` at the pinned authority
`noisemaker@403c2a4b`) runs the tiled pipeline math
`sampledLocalUV = fract((wrappedGlobalUV * fullResolution - tileOffset) / dims)`.
With no tiling (fullResolution == dims, tileOffset == 0) that is
`fract(applyWrap(uv * scale))` — an extra fract AFTER the wrap. It is a no-op
for mirror/repeat (both already land in [0,1)) but maps the CLAMP branch's
exact 1.0 back to 0.0, so clamped samples hit texel 0, not the last texel.
The checked-in `wgsl/reverb.wgsl` lacks that trailing fract, and the Unity port
had implemented the WGSL verbatim — hence only `wrap=clamp` diverged.

## Probe isolation (256px, iterations=1, licensed Unity 6000.3.16f1 darwin/arm64)

Single-iteration probes (`noise(seed:1,ridges:true).reverb(wrap:clamp|repeat,
iterations:1)`) solved the per-pixel scaled-sample texel from
`scaled = (result*1.5 - original)*2` on both sides:

- BEFORE the fix the port's clamped samples hit the LAST texel (e.g. png
  (row 10, col 250) -> texel (10,255)), the golden's hit texel 0 in the GL
  bottom-up frame — matching the `fract`-after-clamp model exactly.
- AFTER the fix (`frac()` applied to the clamp branch output in
  `Shaders/Effects/filter/Reverb.hlsl`):

```
probe-noise   port-vs-gold max 1 mean 0.052
probe-clamp1  port-vs-gold max 1 mean 0.084
probe-repeat1 port-vs-gold max 1 mean 0.086
```
